What We Care About

Our Commitment to the Region

✓
Environmental Stewardship Trees are our livelihood, and we don't take that lightly. We recommend removal only when it's genuinely necessary, and we're glad to talk through pruning or treatment options that keep a healthy tree standing wherever possible.
✓
Safer Neighbourhoods Hazardous trees put more than a single property at risk. Our SAFE-certified, WorkSafeBC-compliant crews take the same care with the neighbours, the street, and the surrounding property as we do with the job itself.
✓
Training the Next Generation Arboriculture is a skilled trade. We invest in our crew's ISA certification and ongoing training, which means more qualified arborists working across the region — not just at Silverback.
✓
Sea to Sky Roots We work the steep terrain, coastal weather, and wildfire-interface concerns of this region every day. That local knowledge — not a generic playbook — is what shapes every recommendation we make.
